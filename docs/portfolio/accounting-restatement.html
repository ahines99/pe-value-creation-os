The diligence decision

Reopen the earnings baseline when reliance is withdrawn. Keep the original record for audit history, suspend its use in a current decision, and review the correction before accepting a replacement baseline.

FY2005 operating income changes from $63.765 million to $59.950 million; net income changes from $48.933 million to $46.257 million. Operating cash flow remains $80.630 million. An earnings correction is not automatically a cash outflow or an EBITDA add-back.

Cash and equivalents change by $-17.001 million, with an offsetting short-term-investment reclassification. Combined cash and investments are unchanged. This is not an operating cash-saving initiative.
