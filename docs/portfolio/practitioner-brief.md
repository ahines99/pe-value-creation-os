# A 35-minute practitioner review

Please challenge whether this portfolio project supports a useful PE operating
decision. It combines Progress Software public financial disclosures with a
separate constructed operating exercise. It is not a Progress engagement and
claims no actual company savings. No confidential records are requested.

Start at the [executive memo](decision-memo.html), then use its evidence and
supporting exhibits. The [case study](case-study.md) provides context if needed.
The [release record](https://github.com/ahines99/pe-value-creation-os/pull/52)
identifies the version being presented; the presenter should record that version
before the session. Please note if you reviewed a later version.

## Your review

Spend the first five minutes reading without presenter explanation. Then discuss
the following questions. There are no required positive answers; a clear reason
to stop or change the work is useful feedback.

1. In your own words, what decision is being supported? What, if anything, would
   you authorize now? What needs further evidence?
2. Choose one headline figure. Can you trace it to its inputs, source and
   calculation? Identify whether the inputs are public observations, constructed
   examples or assumptions. Record where you lose the trail, if anywhere.
3. Does the proposed mechanism distinguish earnings, cash and value? Identify
   any unsupported adjustment, overlap or misleading comparison you see.
4. Challenge one assumption or source. Before the presenter reruns anything,
   state what you expect to change and why. Compare that expectation with the
   actual result; disagreement should remain in the record.
5. What is your strongest objection? Would you stop, revise or pursue further
   diligence? Name the next piece of evidence you would request.

Allow roughly ten minutes for financial/commercial questions, fifteen for two
specific challenges and five for your closing judgment. Assess only areas within
your experience. You do not need to provide an endorsement or numerical score.

## Record your feedback

The presenter maintains the [response log](practitioner-review.md#assessment-and-response-log).
Before retaining responses, agree whether they may be stored privately, quoted
anonymously or attributed publicly. Sharing a comment is not permission to
publish your name. Include your relevant professional role, review scope and
any relationship to the author that affects independence.

For each objection, record your actual comment, the affected figure or page,
the proposed correction and whether the correction addresses your concern.
Unresolved objections stay visible. Participation establishes only the review
actually performed; it does not certify normalized earnings, company outcomes,
production readiness or every feature of the software.
