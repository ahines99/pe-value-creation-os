Research judgment / No operating approval

What the evidence changes

These are authored research assessments. They do not approve an intervention, assign company staff or establish financial value.

Does reported growth demonstrate improvement in the pre-ShareFile business?

Investigate · The disclosed ShareFile contribution increase exceeds consolidated revenue growth. The remaining decline merits explanation before a recovery thesis is underwritten.

Mechanism and population: Separate perimeter changes from price, volume, renewal timing and currency. Consolidated FY2024/FY2025 revenue, with ShareFile removed arithmetically.

Supporting context: sharefile-contribution. Counterevidence / limits: renewal-timing · nuclia-perimeter · currency-definition.

Alternative explanations: Renewal timing; Currency movement; Other acquisition contribution; Product and service mix.

Evidence needed: Product-level revenue bridge on a constant perimeter; Renewal calendar and comparable quantities; Nuclia contribution and constant-currency amounts.

Test that changes the decision: Reject a structural deterioration or recovery claim if a reconciled timing/perimeter bridge explains the residual without an underlying change; retain any unresolved remainder.

Research author assessment · review by 2026-10-31 · ID growth-quality

Can the license decline justify a broad price-book migration?

Defer · The public figures do not establish eligible contracts, enforceable uplifts or net retention economics.

Mechanism and population: Test price realization only within comparable, contractually eligible renewal cohorts. No verified customer cohort is available.

Supporting context: revenue-definition. Counterevidence / limits: renewal-timing.

Alternative explanations: Multi-year renewal timing; Quantity and product mix; Channel economics rather than underpricing.

Evidence needed: Contracts, notice periods, caps and channel terms; Matched invoices, concessions, quantities and renewal outcomes; Churn and contribution margin by eligible cohort.

Test that changes the decision: Reject the price intervention if the apparent gap disappears after mix/quantity adjustments, contracts prohibit it, or lost contribution and implementation costs exceed the uplift.

Research author assessment · review by 2026-10-31 · ID pricing-headroom

Can ending ShareFile transition services create new savings?

Reject · The disclosed agreement already ended in Q2 FY2025. Treating its termination as a new intervention would count an existing action again.

Mechanism and population: Remove a continuing transition-service obligation only if it still exists. The disclosed ShareFile TSA.

Supporting context: cost-perimeter. Counterevidence / limits: tsa-completed.

Alternative explanations: Post-TSA replacement costs may continue under different arrangements.

Evidence needed: A separately documented continuing obligation and current approved budget, if any.

Test that changes the decision: Reopen only for a distinct, documented remaining obligation with an owner, termination right and incremental cost action; never reuse the completed TSA termination.

Research author assessment · review by 2026-10-31 · ID tsa-cancellation

Can acquisition costs establish an automation or integration savings pool?

Defer · Consolidated cost growth and acquisition revenue do not identify removable operating costs or standalone acquired earnings.

Mechanism and population: Link a specific workflow or duplicate obligation to a safe operating change and an actual spend action. No verified ticket, staffing, vendor or duplicate-cost population is available.

Supporting context: cost-perimeter. Counterevidence / limits: earnings-unavailable · tsa-completed.

Alternative explanations: Costs may support a larger acquired business; Integrated shared costs may remain after process changes; Costs may already be removed in the baseline.

Evidence needed: Reconciled cost center and vendor ledger; Ticket volumes, quality constraints and staffing commitments; Existing integration plan and actions already in the budget.

Test that changes the decision: Reject if safe capacity release does not permit a supported spend action, quality deteriorates, or the same cost is already removed or claimed elsewhere.
