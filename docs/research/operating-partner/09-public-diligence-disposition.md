# Public diligence: final research dispositions

Research disposition dated September 29, 2026 (America/New_York), retaining the
case's September 28, 2026 information cutoff. This is an author assessment of
public evidence, not independent finance approval or a Progress engagement.

The recommendation is bounded further diligence. Public facts establish a change
in the consolidation perimeter and several reasons to challenge a simple growth
or margin story. They do not establish an executable company savings program.
The separate constructed operating exercise demonstrates the decision workflow.

## Acquisition perimeter and growth quality

The primary anchor is the [FY2025 Form 10-K](https://investors.progress.com/static-files/2a74fac9-6513-42b2-b875-51cb2da6021c),
particularly MD&A and Note 5. The checked input register is
[growth-context.json](../../../data/public/progress/growth-context.json), including
page locators, source identity, contrary evidence and reopening tests.

| Question | Public evidence and research disposition | Effect on the decision / evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Is FY2025 growth organic? | Consolidated revenue rose from $753.409m to $977.831m. Disclosed ShareFile contributions are approximately $21.1m and $261.6m, covering one month and a full year respectively. | Investigate. The approximate $240.5m contribution increase exceeds the $224.422m consolidated increase. The approximate −$16.078m remainder is an arithmetic non-ShareFile change, not organic decline. |

| Is acquired-business profitability known? | Standalone ShareFile earnings are not separately provided after integration. | No invented product P&L, acquired EBITDA or synergy capture. Require reconciled management accounts and allocation policies. |
| Can ending transition services create new savings? | The ShareFile transition-services agreement already ended in Q2 FY2025. | Reject that specific future savings thesis. Reopen only for a distinct continuing obligation, never the completed termination. |
| Does cost growth identify redundant resources? | The filing connects increased costs to the acquired operating perimeter and hosting/headcount. | Defer cost-out sizing until cost-center, vendor, workload and quality evidence establish an incremental spend action. |

The period boundaries matter as much as the arithmetic. The
[September 22, 2026 Domo filing](https://investors.progress.com/node/28981/html)
is retained as a subsequent acquisition event. It does not retroactively change
the FY2025 earnings or debt/cash anchor. Historical sensitivities are not current
post-acquisition transaction values.

## Peer comparability

The four selected candidates have mapped public statements; the
[peer disposition](07-peer-evidence-review.md) records the inclusion/exclusion
effects. Strict operating-margin eligibility has one candidate, while strict
cash eligibility has none. The existing three-observation minimum therefore
withholds both strict medians.

Different business mix, investment capitalization, acquisition timing and metric
definitions prevent interpreting contextual ratios as an achievable operating
target. No savings pool is inferred from the gap to a contextual median. A usable
comparison needs matched populations, common investment definitions and a
reconciled perimeter; adding weak peers merely to reach the threshold is rejected.
