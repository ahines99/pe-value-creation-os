# Value Creation OS: proposed operating pilot

**Discussion draft · no sponsor appointed · pilot not started**

## The decision this pilot supports

Can an evidence-linked workflow help your team choose one feasible operating
improvement, understand its earnings and cash consequences, and judge the result?
The pilot tests that question within one company and one operating lever. It does
not promise savings or require a positive investment case to count as useful.

## What we propose

Start with a six-week diagnostic and review trial after the readiness gates are
met. Select one lever for which a finance reviewer and accountable operator can
validate the records: renewal pricing, service operations, or collections.
Use scoped, read-only exports to establish the baseline, review two diagnostics,
and decide whether a limited intervention merits authorization.

An authorized intervention can then use a separate operating review through day
100, with its own start date, capacity, spending limit and stopping rules. A
six-week diagnostic does not establish realized financial benefits. Timing of
renewals, invoices and cost actions may require a longer observation window.

## What your team receives

- A reconciled baseline with missing records and exclusions made visible.
- A short decision memo comparing proceed, revise, defer and stop.
- An explicit bridge from operating changes to EBITDA and cash, including costs.
- A feasible plan with accountable owners, capacity and dependencies.
- If an intervention is authorized, an original/current/actual review with
  alternative explanations and unattributed residuals retained.

Management retains all decision rights. The application does not change customer
prices, contact customers, change staffing or write to operating systems.

## What participation requires

An authorized sponsor, a finance/data owner, and an operator must agree on scope,
validate the baseline, review the proposed action and assess the outcome. One
person may fill more than one role if their authority and capacity allow it.
The project team prepares mappings, reconciliations, analysis and meeting materials.
Meeting dates and participant effort are agreed at kickoff rather than assumed.

Private records remain outside the public portfolio. Access, processing,
retention and any publication require separately documented permissions. The
current public demonstration uses public filings and constructed operating
records; it has no company sponsor, accepted intervention or measured company
impact. The permissioned ingestion lane and applicable readiness controls still
need implementation and verification before private records are received.

## First conversation

A proposed 30-minute discussion covers the decision you want to make, the single
lever worth investigating, who can validate its records and authorize action,
and what would make the pilot stop. No customer export or credentials are needed
for that discussion.

The [executive demonstration](../../portfolio/decision-memo.html) shows how an
initially attractive constructed case becomes adverse when operating evidence
changes. It illustrates the workflow, not expected results for your company.
