to 59,950; net income from 48,933 to 46,257. The net-income bridge is
48,933 - 2,611 stock compensation - 1,204 payroll withholding + 1,139 tax benefit

209,021 to 226,022; their combined 266,420 is unchanged. Opening cash changes by
-11,500; investing cash flow changes by -5,500 and the exchange-rate effect by

These are accounting corrections and classifications. The comparison does not
establish an EBITDA adjustment, cash saving or value-creation result.
