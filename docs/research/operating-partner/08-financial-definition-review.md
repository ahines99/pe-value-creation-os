## Earnings and adjustment decisions

The existing FY2025 net-income-based bridge is, in USD millions:

`73.133 net income + 8.495 tax + 70.850 interest + 6.232 PP&E D&A + 145.492 amortization = 304.202`.

The separate operating-income bridge is `153.290 + 6.232 + 145.492 = 305.014`.
Their 0.812 difference is the retained non-interest, non-tax non-operating effect.
Neither is issuer-adjusted or normalized maintainable EBITDA.

| Item | Treatment | Evidence needed to change treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Stock compensation | Retain economic compensation cost; no automatic cash benefit | Sustainable compensation/replacement-pay and dilution analysis |
| Restructuring | Retain expense | Program-level recurring-cost history, remaining commitments and finance review |
| Acquisition expense | Retain expense in an acquisitive operating model | Transaction-level invoices, integration costs and maintainability assessment |
| Cyber response | Retain expense | Recovery reconciliation and continuing remediation/security requirements |
| Acquired-intangible D&A | Already included where reconciled | No second addback; resolve scope before using disputed periods |
| Debt-cost amortization | Already contained in the interest adjustment | No second addback; keep financing cash and principal separate |

The FY2025 restructuring note (page 65) records activity in all three years and
commitments beyond year end. It supports retaining the expense, rather than
assuming its label proves it is nonrecurring. Expense recognition and cash payment
are different events; a reserve balance is not an incremental savings opportunity.

## Cash, implementation and valuation boundaries

| Measure or assumption | Public-case treatment |
|---|---|
| Historical CFO less PP&E | FY2025 235.187 − 5.702 = 229.485 million; a defined cash proxy, not discretionary cash or initiative savings |
| Operating implementation expense | Reduces modeled period EBITDA and cash according to its own schedule |
| Capitalized implementation spending | Affects modeled cash separately; no automatic EBITDA saving or unprovided depreciation schedule |
| Collections / working capital | Timing-related cash effect; no EBITDA benefit without a separate supported mechanism |
| Financing and debt service | Separate from operating uplift; principal, interest and transaction claims are not mixed into a savings bridge |
| Historical valuation | Same-date November 30, 2025 earnings/balances with explicit 4×/6×/8×/10× author sensitivities |
| Current equity value / transaction proceeds | Unavailable: historical balances predate Domo, and actual payoff, conversion, cash restrictions, tax and fees are not established |
| Constructed operating uplift | Kept separate from real company earnings and historical company valuation |

The mechanical historical sensitivity remains available because its FY2025
earnings anchor reconciles. It does not repair earlier-period earnings, establish
a market multiple, or establish maintainability. No normalized earnings series
or current transaction value is accepted by this review.
