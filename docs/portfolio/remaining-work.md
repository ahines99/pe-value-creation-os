## Portfolio completion

### 1. Reconcile the completion inventory — Codex

**Current status:** implementation through PR #50 is released and verified.
The private-review workspace below has a separate release gate; its
local presence does not establish publication or pilot readiness. Historical counts
remain attached to their original builds in this checklist and the acceptance map.

Maintain one requirement map that distinguishes implemented behavior, unresolved
research, unavailable evidence and external dependencies. Historical release counts
must retain their build identity. The original OP-16 publication-time/restatement
criterion must remain visible until actually satisfied.

**Done when:** the [acceptance map](progress-acceptance.md), this checklist and the
roadmap agree on current scope and released functionality. New work updates them
together; no test result is promoted to a business-validation claim.

### 2. Finish the public-company diligence — Codex

Recheck source inventory and the declared information cutoff. Complete the
acquisition/perimeter and organic-growth analysis where disclosures support it.
Separate publicly reported facts, inference, modeling assumptions and unknown
operating information. Preserve source identity, period, units and provenance.

**Deliverable:** final public evidence and diligence-gap register.
**Done when:** every material conclusion has supporting evidence or a visible
limitation and a stated effect on the recommendation. Missing customer economics
or product profitability is not replaced with fabricated company data.

### 3. Finish the financial-definition review — Codex

**Public-source disposition recorded:** the [definition review](../research/operating-partner/08-financial-definition-review.md)
reconciles supported measures and records all four annual/interim amortization
exceptions, plus their derived-quarter effect. Both executive adjustment registers
include every supplied exception. The cause remains unavailable; dependent
earnings stay withheld. Component evidence, maintainability and independent finance
approval are still outstanding, with specific requests recorded in the review.

Investigate comparative acquired-intangible/other-amortization differences between
cash-flow and income-statement measures. Complete the earnings adjustment rationale
and distinguish recurring economics, implementation expense, capex, working capital,
financing and transaction assumptions. Recheck dependent valuation eligibility.

**Deliverable:** reconciled definition/adjustment register and unresolved-items log.
**Done when:** supported earnings measures reconcile; unsupported measures and
dependent valuations remain withheld. Maintainability judgments are explicit and
internal analysis is not described as independent finance approval.

### 4. Finish peer and thesis decisions — Codex

**Source review:** all four selected candidates now have verified financial
mappings, including SS&C. The [disposition](../research/operating-partner/07-peer-evidence-review.md)
records why broader context is available but strict medians remain withheld.

Review candidate peers by metric, business mix, period and accounting definition.
Show how exclusions change or suppress comparisons. For each thesis, finalize
retain/reject/defer, mechanism, contrary evidence, falsification test and next
data request. A public margin difference alone does not establish addressable
cost savings.

**Deliverable:** peer eligibility and thesis decision register.
**Done when:** the recommended investigation follows the evidence; unsupported
opportunities stay unsized and adverse findings remain visible.

### 5. Complete the historical replay evidence — Codex

**Current increment:** the [FY2005 accounting correction](accounting-restatement.html)
and intervening non-reliance chronology are released with verified CI and
publication. Exact public-availability timing remains unverified.

Locate and verify an original disclosure and an actual later accounting
restatement. Preserve both information sets and any intervening non-reliance
notice. Establish defensible publication timing before making a point-in-time
availability claim; SEC acceptance alone does not establish first public access.

**Deliverable:** sourced original/corrected comparison, chronology and cutoff tests.
**Done when:** a historical cutoff cannot read later facts, the original snapshot
remains intact and the required timing is supported. The existing ShareFile
measurement-period adjustment is a different event. If timing or original-source
evidence cannot be obtained, OP-16 remains explicitly unsatisfied; a limitation
note alone does not close this requirement.

### 6. Consolidate the executive walkthrough — Codex

**Published in PR #40:** the landing page now leads with the Progress
decision and a six-stop primary route. The README and presenter guide follow the
same sequence. Shared-pool examples and the fictional local approval demo are
explicitly separate; deeper exhibits remain available through an expandable index.
Browser checks verify route destinations and bind the landing conclusion to the
saved memo's reopened preference and authority state. Publication acceptance is
verified; actual observed executive comprehension remains unperformed.

Create a clear primary route through business context, thesis, evidence, explicit
assumptions, EBITDA/cash/valuation, constrained first-wave selection, source
challenge, revised decision and realization/KPI history. Keep technical exhibits
available without making them the executive entry point. Clearly distinguish the
real public company from the constructed operating examples.

**Deliverable:** coherent executive route and a short guided demonstration.
**Done when:** a reader can trace a headline figure, understand the next decision
and see what would change it. Summaries match their saved versions; distinct
examples are not presented as one actual company engagement.

### 7. Finalize portfolio packaging — Codex

Update the landing page, README, case study, architecture explanation, setup guide,

source-data rights and keep credentials and restricted extracts private.

**Deliverable:** consistent public project and interview package.
**Done when:** the public claims match demonstrated capabilities, an unfamiliar
reviewer has an obvious starting point and setup instructions are reproducible.
No synthetic result is described as realized client EBITDA or an actual deal.

### 8. Run final showcase acceptance and publish — Codex

Rebuild from permitted inputs in a clean environment. Run checks appropriate to
the final code changes, including numerical/adversarial cases, source and artifact
reproduction, authentication and approval boundaries, package/Compose operation,
responsive and keyboard review, and secret/export checks. Fix failures, release
the tested version and verify published artifacts against it.

**Deliverable:** final release with an exact-build acceptance receipt and named
limitations. **Done when:** the portfolio requirements are satisfied and verified;
any genuinely unmet criterion remains open rather than silently waived.
This is the polished-showcase stopping point, separate from pilot completion.

### 9. Obtain practitioner challenge when available — Codex prepares; Alex introduces

Prepare a short review packet and specific commercial/calculation questions.
Record actual reviewer identity, reviewed version, objections and responses;
implement corrections and repeat affected checks.

**Done when:** a real reviewer has participated and the resulting feedback is
recorded. An introduction is useful but not required to continue engineering or
publish an honestly labeled showcase. Independent-review and observed-human-
comprehension claims remain unavailable until that review occurs.

**Released in PR #44:** [private financial snapshots](../pilot/permissioned/financial-snapshots.md)

Its CI/publication acceptance is verified above. The
[private underwriting increment](../pilot/permissioned/underwriting.md) is released

are released in PR #46. [Reviewed baselines](../pilot/permissioned/reviewed-baselines.md)
are released in PR #47. The [private-observation increment](../pilot/permissioned/observations.md)

separate release gate. Private operating-source and delivery-cost reconciliation,
executive memo and remaining pilot screens remain open, as do per-source expiry deletion,
legal holds, backup/copy disposal and pilot readiness.

Use a distinct permissioned-input contract and processing/export boundary. Add
source mapping, reconciliation/quarantine, scoped access, correction history,
retention/deletion behavior and review semantics. Test with permitted fixtures
before handling real company records. Adapt company-specific controls after
scope is agreed; do not bypass constructed-only contracts by relabeling records.

**Deliverable:** tested private ingestion and review workflow plus a readiness
checklist. **Done when:** accepted records can be traced and reconciled, invalid
or unauthorized records are rejected and private inputs cannot enter public
exports. This generic work can proceed without a sponsor.

### 11. Find an authorized sponsor — Alex, supported by Codex

Introduce a willing company/data owner who can authorize a bounded pilot and name
a finance reviewer and operating owner. Codex prepares the sponsor brief, meeting
agenda and minimum data request. No outreach is sent without explicit instructions.

**Done when:** a real sponsor agrees to scope discussions and the accountable
reviewers are identified. University LSEG/WRDS access supports research; it does
not establish permission to access a company's private operating records.

### 12. Agree the pilot charter — Codex drafts; company approves

Select one lever and population. Record baseline period, diagnostic deliverables,
success measures, counterfactual, stop/rollback conditions, available operator
capacity and review cadence. Specify authorized sources, processing, access,
retention and permitted outputs. Keep the diagnostic and subsequent intervention
authorization separate.

**Done when:** the sponsor and accountable reviewers approve the bounded scope
and responsibilities. Pilot timing is estimated from that scope, not from the
old engineering roadmap.

### 13. Ingest and reconcile authorized records — Codex implements; company validates

Map source systems, validate coverage, duplicates, credits, cutoffs and units,
reconcile relevant totals to finance and quarantine unresolved exceptions.
Preserve exact source versions and keep records out of the public showcase.

**Deliverable:** finance-reviewed baseline and data-quality exceptions.
**Done when:** inputs needed for the selected intervention are accepted and any
remaining gaps have an explicit effect on what can be calculated or claimed.

### 14. Calibrate and approve the intervention — Codex calculates; company decides

Replace constructed inputs with validated company evidence. Verify applicable
contract/vendor constraints and actual capacity; review downside, cost, timing
and measurement design. Freeze the approved baseline, scope and review version.

**Done when:** named finance and operating reviewers authorize the bounded plan.
A model cannot authorize pricing, staffing or other operating changes.

### 15. Execute and observe — Company operates; Codex supports

Record work, acceptance, costs, exceptions, KPI observations and steering
decisions. Apply stop/rollback conditions when triggered and collect sufficient
real operating periods. Codex maintains the reporting and calculations; the
company performs and authorizes the business activity.

**Done when:** actual execution and observations exist for the agreed evaluation
period. Software cannot manufacture elapsed time or management participation.

### 16. Reconcile realization and close the pilot — Codex prepares; finance/sponsor reviews

Compare observations with the frozen baseline and counterfactual. Separate EBITDA,
cash, costs and timing; examine alternative explanations and retain unattributed
residuals. Prepare a continue/revise/stop recommendation and closeout report.
Publish a real-case summary only within granted rights.

**Done when:** the sponsor/finance review is recorded and supported results and
attribution limits are explicit. Positive, negative and inconclusive outcomes are
all valid. A prepared package or simulation does not satisfy a performed pilot.
